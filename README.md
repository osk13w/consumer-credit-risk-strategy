# Consumer Credit Risk Strategy

A consumer lending credit risk project focused on portfolio analysis, default prediction, risk segmentation, and credit strategy optimization.

## Project Goals

- Analyze consumer loan portfolio performance
- Identify key drivers of credit risk
- Build a probability of default model
- Develop and test credit risk strategies
- Evaluate risk and approval-rate tradeoffs
- Present findings through dashboards and recommendations
